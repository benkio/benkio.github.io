A brief introduction: 167cm (5'6"), 62-72 kg. Just another human trying to navigate life.

Activities and Interests

I'm curious about everything that exists: I might jump from mythology to physics, geopolitics, economics, etc. However, my main interests remain music and tech. Recently, I started learning chess and Japanese, let's see how long I'll resist. My memory is filled with internet trash at this point, and it has eaten up the whole capacity, but there's not much I can do about it. That's what sticks 😄

I spend my free time mainly playing electric guitar, doing coding challenges, working on personal projects, working out, and hanging out with friends. I'm always looking forward to the next metal gig or festival, searching for new bands to listen to, books to read, and movies/TV series to watch. There are too many things to do, and nothing gets done in the end 😄

I used to play basketball, with limited success. I enjoy going on hikes or long walks outside, as well as visiting museums. I'm Italian, but I can't cook at all.

Regarding alcohol, I have a low tolerance. After a pint or two, I'm already at my limit. When tipsy or altered, I don't experience the euphoria people normally seek when drinking—I just slow down, and that's not particularly funny for me or for those around me. If I exceed a certain threshold, I go totally wasted, which is even worse.

If you see someone chasing cats and making weird noises, it's probably me.

Personality
Social Behavior

I'm generally friendly and calm; it's hard to upset me, as I deal with software every day, and you can't compete with that. I am an introvert, so don't expect me to be the center of the party. In group environments, I tend to stay quiet and listen rather than seek the spotlight, though I always try to add something to a conversation if possible. The louder the place, the quieter I become.

I have very few boundaries; you can do and say pretty much anything to me. I rarely take offense, and others can feel free around me. However, I also tend to forget about other people's boundaries, even if they tell me 😅, which can make me come across as disrespectful or upset others unintentionally.

Communication Style

I might come across as awkward at first (something I want to improve on), mainly because I don't know you yet: what you like, wish, want or not, what makes you happy or sad, your past, aspirations… So I don't know what to say. Eventually, with time, I'll fine-tune and start to make ad hoc jokes or references, similar to how a social media algorithm works. Expect me to often respond to jokes seriously: I do recognize they are jokes, but I like to consider them real and keep the joke/conversation alive, adding something related to that.

I tend to focus on problem-solving, which means I often dive into your problems in an attempt to solve them. This happens even when it's not requested, as I interpret a complaint, for instance, as a problem to be solved and analyzed. In such moments, I think I'm being helpful because, as a friend, I feel the duty to improve the other person's situation and life. However, nobody, especially sensitive people, likes to hear from someone else what their flaws or problems are. As a result, the other person may feel bad, criticized, inferior, and inadequate. This is one of the main reasons for conflicts and one of the main areas of improvement for me.

Related to this, I tend to ask a lot of questions until I have every detail about what you are doing, why, and how. That's because, in my mind, the more I know, the better I can help the other person. Still, this can also increase or cause more stress for the other person. When I'm with the right person, I can have long conversations about several topics and talk for hours trying to analyse all possible aspects.

When talking, I'm very spontaneous, so I don't spend much time thinking about the effects of what I say. Add to this the fact that I never have anxiety and I struggle to identify what causes it. As a result, I might do or say things that make you anxious or embarrass you in public, especially if in an altered state (e.g., tired).

I often need to have a plan to follow if I'm going to do something, and probably a backup plan as well. This applies also to normal interactions: I like to have a topic instead of improvising. I can't do a call without knowing what we will talk about, and I hate calls in general. Everything needs to have a purpose. This connects with that initial awkwardness I mentioned. I tend to start collecting data on you at some point, like what gifts you might like so I don't disappoint you, or open topics we didn't properly finish talking about. In those rare cases when I go with the flow, I'm in a controlled environment where I know that making mistakes is not the end of the world.

Values & Approach

I'll always be the devil's advocate, trying to find the most positive explanation for other people's actions. So, if you expect me to vent together against an enemy of yours, it probably won't happen 😄

I dislike waste and inefficiency. I'll probably keep using something broken until it's unusable or try to fix it before throwing it away. This also makes me go for cheap stuff because spending more on something that's crafted in China anyway is a waste of money. As a result, I live on a budget even if I can afford more, but I prefer to save for the future. I will always accommodate someone else over my own desires. Moreover, I don't expect anything from others, so if I get ghosted or mistreated, I won't be particularly sad; on the contrary, I think it's the right thing to do.

Self-Perception

I tend to focus on what's wrong and what can be improved, even when I've reached good results. I think that's partly due to how my work is. Always looking for problems to solve.

I'm often late and struggle with time management.

Emotional Side

First things first: #TeamBoobs ( . 人 . )

I've never fallen in love, and at this point and it's not a big deal. I don't think it's possible, as I immediately see the defects of the person I have in front of me, so I don't have that initial idealisation phase. No butterflies in the stomach. However, not all hope is lost 😄, as I get attached very quickly to people (unless in rare cases), and I like to form strong bonds with them. I try to be as supportive as possible and help my friends beyond what's usually done or healthy.

I don't have space or time for everyone, so a handful of people end up being selected as close friends. Those can ask me whatever, and I'll do whatever it takes to make sure they are happy. Of course, I would like to see something in return, like some appreciation and affection. If I don't, I might be sad at first (I'm not a robot after all), but then I'll cool down on my own.

I'm not jealous by any means, and I don't understand jealousy. I value freedom over everything, as I want it as well, and I get upset if it is constrained. In return, I won't impose constraints of any kind. I think this also links with the trust between each other.

I'm aromantic, so don't expect me to dive into romantic gestures or signs of affection through either words or actions. As listed below, I show my affection in another way, especially by trying to improve your life and being at your service.

I'm a highly independent person, so don't expect me to always think about you all the time or ask for support. Rather, I might disappear from time to time to focus on my passions, duties, and my own mental health. This doesn't mean I don't care about the other person; I just like to have my own time.
